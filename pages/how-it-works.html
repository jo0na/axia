How Axia Works

Learn how to bring your chosen content together.

  1. 01 Choose your purpose

    Decide whether you are creating something for yourself, someone else, or both.

  2. 02 Add your content

    Include letters, photos, videos, songs, notes, recommendations, coupons, and more.

  3. 03 Personalize it

    Arrange the contents and customize the appearance of your collection.

  4. 04 Preview and save

    Review it, then save it to your account or continue without signing in.

  5. 05 Share it when you’re ready

    Generate a link if you want someone else to open it.
