About the website

Create collections
that fit your needs.

Axia lets you collect letters, stories, notes, photos, recommendations, and other digital items in one organized space. You can make a collection for yourself, or put one together for someone who needs a little encouragement.

No need to send eight separate links and hope they find everything.

Our Mission

Sometimes you want to help, remember, recommend, or say something meaningful, but the contents end up scattered across chats, galleries, notes, and saved links.

Axia brings them together so you can arrange them with purpose and share them in one place.

Our Values

  • Thoughtfulness

    We value the care behind every collection

  • Creativity

    There is more than one way to write, recommend, remember, or encourage.

  • Trust

    We keep your memories and personal content safe.

  • Connection

    A collection can be useful to you or meaningful to someone else.

Because what you share, stays with them.
